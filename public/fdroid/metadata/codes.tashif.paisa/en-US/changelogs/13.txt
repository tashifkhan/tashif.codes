A floating navbar that sits over your list, a way to add a category without leaving the picker, and motion across the app.

* The list shows behind the bar.
* Tab titles. A new toggle in More → Appearance puts a title under every tab.
* Frosted glass. A second toggle blurs and tints what scrolls behind the bar.
* The bar picks up a little of your accent colour, and on light themes its shadow does too.
* It moves. One pill slides between tabs.
* Add a category from the picker.
